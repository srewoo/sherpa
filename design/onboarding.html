How answers are generated

Sherpa always grounds answers in your indexed pages. Choose which model writes the answer. You can change this any time in Settings.

No key needed. If you don't add one, Chrome's built-in model is used. An Extractive tier (no model at all — verbatim passages with citations) is always available as a fallback and works even offline.
